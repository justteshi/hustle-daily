Visual direction

Calm, athletic and precise

Warm paper ground, white surfaces, green-black ink and one natural green accent. Numbers carry the hierarchy: big tabular figures with the unit right beside them. Each category gets one accessible hue (food, movement, sleep, energy) that is used only for small marks and tints.

Use: quiet surfaces · strong numerals · labelled status · thin bars Avoid: neon · glass · rings everywhere · streaks · red for “missed”
